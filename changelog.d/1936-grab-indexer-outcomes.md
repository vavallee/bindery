### Changed
- **An automatic grab now records when part of the indexer pool never answered** (#1936) — a failing indexer returns no results, which looked exactly like an indexer that had nothing to offer. So if two of your three indexers were down, auto-grab quietly picked the best of whatever the third returned and nothing anywhere said that most of the pool was never asked. If the release you wanted was on one of the other two, it just did not get grabbed. Grabs decided this way are now stamped with how many indexers failed and why, visible in History, and it is logged rather than buried at debug level. What gets grabbed is deliberately unchanged: this makes the decision auditable first, so that acting on the signal can be a separate and deliberate choice.
