### Fixed
- **Usenet job folders no longer pile up after import** (#3647). When a SABnzbd or NZBGet release came with extras such as an .nfo, a cover .jpg or a .url, the job folder stayed in the completed folder forever after the book was moved into the library. Once every book file in the job has imported, Bindery now deletes those extras and the folder. A folder holding anything else, such as a book that was not imported or a file type it does not recognise, is kept and the log names the file. Torrent downloads, partial imports, copy mode and the download client's own completed and category folders are never touched.
