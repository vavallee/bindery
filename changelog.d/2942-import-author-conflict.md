### Fixed
- **Import no longer offers a look alike by the wrong author** (#2942). When audio files sit in another author's folder, Import, In your library now reads the author and book from their tags or track names, says so on the row ("Files say Katy Evans, folder says James Patterson"), and suggests that author's books first, scored on the book the files name. The folder author's books are still listed but never preselected or offered as a one click adopt, and a title that only shares its opening letters with an unrelated one no longer shows up as an 80% match.
