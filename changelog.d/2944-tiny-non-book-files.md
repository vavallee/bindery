### Fixed
- **Tiny notes files are no longer offered as books** (#2944). Import, In your library listed 1 KB `.txt` files as ebooks, suggested the book their folder named, and adopting one made it that book's ebook. An ebook format file under 4 KiB is now listed on its own row as too small to be a book, with no suggestion and Ignore as its action, and adopting it is refused. When your audiobooks folder is separate from your library, a row found in the other format's folder (an ebook under the audiobooks root, say) is labelled with that folder and is never a one click Confirm. Files already tracked are not touched. Thanks to foobarbigtime for the report.
