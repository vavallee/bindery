### Added
- **Released and upcoming scope for the Series page's Missing filter** (#2871). With Missing books selected you can now count only released books, so a volume that is not out yet no longer makes a series look incomplete, or only upcoming ones. A book counts as released when its release date is today or earlier; a book with no date, like a working title volume, counts as upcoming. The card badge follows the scope while it is on. Hardcover books you do not have yet join the scoped counts once the series has been opened, since that is when their dates load. The scope is kept in the URL, and like the other filters it never marks, monitors or searches anything.
