### Fixed
- **An indexer's error page no longer fails the grab under SABnzbd's name** (#2105) — SABnzbd and NZBGet grabs fetch the NZB from the indexer and hand the download client the bytes, so an indexer that answers a refused, expired or rate-limited grab with HTTP 200 and an error page had that page forwarded to the client. The client refused to parse it and the grab failed with `SABnzbd rejected download`, naming the one component in the chain that behaved correctly, while the book dropped silently back to Wanted. Bindery now checks that the fetched body is actually an NZB before it goes anywhere, and reports what the indexer really sent — including the newznab error code where there is one, and the Prowlarr redirect-hop guidance that already existed for 4xx responses. A failure part-way through reading the body is also named properly now instead of arriving as a bare `context deadline exceeded`. Indexers that serve the NZB as a gzip or bzip2 *file* rather than as a compressed HTTP response are unaffected: those bodies are unwrapped for the check and still handed to the download client exactly as they arrived, since SABnzbd and NZBGet decompress them themselves.
