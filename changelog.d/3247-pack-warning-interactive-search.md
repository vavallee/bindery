### Added
- **Multi-book packs are labelled in interactive search** (#3247). A release that looks like several books now shows a Multi-book pack label with the words it matched, sorts below the single books, and asks before you grab it from the book page or the Wanted page, since Bindery will not import a pack on its own. Automatic search still skips packs. Bindery also recognises three more pack shapes: a range right after the wanted book's title or its series name ("Dominion of Blades 1-2", "[Heart of Texas 01-03]"), and a list of the author's titles separated by semicolons ("The Firm; The Appeal; The Chamber"). A part, disc or track range such as "Dune CD 1-12" and a long track count such as "Dune 1-49" are not packs. Thanks foobarbigtime for the proposal and kennethp1015 for the examples.
