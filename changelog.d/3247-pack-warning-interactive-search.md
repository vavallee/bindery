### Added
- **Multi-book packs are labelled in interactive search** (#3247). A release that looks like several books now shows a Multi-book pack label with the words it matched, sorts below the single books, and asks before you grab it, since Bindery will not import a pack on its own. Automatic search still skips packs. Bindery also recognises three more pack shapes: a range right after the wanted book's title or its series name ("Dominion of Blades 1-2", "[Heart of Texas 01-03]"), and a list of the author's titles separated by semicolons ("The Firm; The Appeal; The Chamber"). Thanks kennethp1015 for the examples.
