### Fixed
- **Torrents that keep failing are now blocklisted, carefully** (#4111). A torrent whose download link answers 404 or 410, or that never reaches your client, was retried every six hours for ever, because only usenet failures and stalls reached the blocklist. Bindery now counts those failures per release and blocklists a release after three in a row, at least an hour apart, but only while its indexer is otherwise serving and no other release from it failed, so a broken indexer never takes good releases down with it. A single failure is still just retried, and a client that is unreachable, a torrent your client reports as errored (usually a full disk) and an indexer 429 or server error never count against a release. When an indexer answers with a 429, a server error or a "disabled till" notice, Bindery leaves that indexer alone for a while instead of re-sending its releases and extending the ban. A dead release alone on a quiet indexer can still need a manual blocklist from the History page. Thanks to highlander-phill for the detailed report.
