### Fixed
- **Torrents that fail over and over are now blocklisted** (#4111). A torrent that failed at grab time or never reached your client was retried every six hours for ever, because only usenet failures and stalls ever reached the blocklist. Bindery now counts those failures per release and blocklists a release after three failures in a row, at least an hour apart, with a reason such as `downloadFailed: indexer HTTP 429, failed 3 times in a row`. A single failure is still just retried, since it is often your client or the indexer having a bad moment, a client that is unreachable never counts against a release, and neither does a torrent your client reports as errored, which is usually a full disk. When an indexer answers with a 429, a server error or a "disabled till" notice, Bindery also leaves that indexer alone for a while instead of re-sending its releases and extending the ban. Thanks to highlander-phill for the detailed report.
