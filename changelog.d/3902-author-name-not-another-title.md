### Fixed

- **Releases named "Title by Author" are no longer filtered out when the library holds a book titled with the author's name.** If an author had a book like "Octavia E. Butler: The Last Interview", the search filter read her own name as another book and hid every "Survivor by Octavia E Butler" release. The author's name, or an alias, is now never treated as another title (#3902). Thanks to digitalbile for the report.
