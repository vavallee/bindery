### Fixed
- **Library scan no longer attaches a file to a different book with a similar title** (#3388): A Dutch Deathly Hallows chapter under a tracked Harry Potter folder was attached to Order of the Phoenix because the two titles share most of their words, and the right book was excluded so nothing else competed. When the best match has its own distinct words and none of them appear in the file's title, the file is now left unmatched on the Import page without that book offered as a close suggestion, and adding a book no longer treats such a file as one you already own. Typos, accents, subtitles and series notes still match. Thanks to foobarbigtime for the report and the reproduction.
