### Fixed
- **BookOrbit import: clearer "book match is ambiguous" failures, and co-authored books now link** (#4189). When a book's file is already tracked on a Bindery book under a different author, the failure now names that book and author instead of only saying the match is ambiguous, and the run logs it. The author check also looks at every author BookOrbit credits, not just the first, so co-authored books and anthologies whose file sits on a book filed under another credited author are linked instead of failing. Near spellings such as "Heather Gray" and "Heather Graham" still count as a conflict. Thanks to @gavinmcfall for the report and the detailed trace.
