### Fixed
- **Releases naming a book's alternate "or" title are no longer filtered out** (#3402): For a one-word title such as `Frankenstein`, releases like `Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus` or `The Hobbit, or There and Back Again` were dropped with "title/author keywords did not match release name", so automatic search could only grab a release using the short title. An `or` after a comma or semicolon, or followed by an article, is now read as the book's alternate title. Releases that run on into a different book, such as `Foundation and Empire` for `Foundation` or `Truth or Dare` for `Truth`, are still rejected, and so is an `or` that leads into another title in your library. Thanks to orshih6 for the fix.
