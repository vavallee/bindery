### Added
- **RanobeDB titles for light novels** (#2012). An opt in metadata source, off by default: when on, a light novel gets its official English title from RanobeDB, but only for a match with a clear best score, the same volume numbers and a matching author, and never over a locked title. Settings → Metadata. RanobeDB's data is ODbL and its API is for non commercial use; see `docs/third-party-data.md`.
