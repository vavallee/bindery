### Fixed
- **Releases a tracker stores with decomposed accents are found** (#3137). Some trackers store a release title decomposed (an `a` followed by a combining ring instead of `å`) and match search terms byte for byte, so every search for such a book came back empty, while searching the author alone found it. When a search finds nothing and the title or author has letters that decompose (å, é, ñ, ö and the like, but not ø or æ), Bindery now runs it once more with the decomposed spelling. The relevance check that decides whether an indexer's answer is about the book also reads a decomposed release title as the composed one.
