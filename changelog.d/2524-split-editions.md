### Fixed
- **Split-edition catalogue rows no longer count as missing books you already own** (#2524): some Hardcover catalogs list a novel's own split-into-parts editions beside the whole novel (The Way of Kings at position 1, with "Part 1" and "Part 2" at 1.1 and 1.2). Already owning the whole novel left those parts listed as missing and offered an add button that queued a duplicate download. They now show in their own collapsed section, linked to the book that covers them, and Fill gaps no longer creates them. Thanks magrhino for the Stormlight and Wheel of Time catalogue data that shaped the rule.
