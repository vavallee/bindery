### Fixed
- **Split-edition catalogue rows no longer count as missing books you don't have** (#2524): some Hardcover catalogs list a novel's own split-into-parts editions alongside the whole novel — Brandon Sanderson's The Way of Kings at position 1, with its "Part 1" and "Part 2" at 1.1 and 1.2. Already owning the whole novel left those parts reading as missing volumes on the Series page, counted against the series and sitting behind an add button that would create and queue a download for text already on the shelf. They now show up in their own collapsed section instead, linked to the book that already covers them, and Fill gaps no longer creates them. A real novella at a neighbouring fractional position, such as Edgedancer beside Words of Radiance, is unaffected, and a series represented only by its split parts, with no unsplit whole to speak of, is unaffected too. Thanks magrhino for the Stormlight and Wheel of Time catalogue data that shaped the rule.
