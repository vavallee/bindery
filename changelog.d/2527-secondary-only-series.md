### Fixed
- **A book whose only series membership is secondary now renames under that series** (#2527) — the renamer looked up the series with a filter on the primary flag, so a book with nothing but secondary memberships got the same empty answer as a book in no series and its files were written with no series folder and no series in the name, while every screen went on showing the series. That happens through the Audiobookshelf sibling catalogue walk, which links books it is not importing without the flag. The flag now breaks ties between memberships instead of suppressing the only one a book has, and the walk gives a sibling with no series at all a primary one.
