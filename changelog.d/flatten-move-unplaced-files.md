### Fixed
- **Flattened audiobook moves no longer delete files they never imported.** With Move as the import mode, a multi-disc flatten or an audiobook file naming template brings over the tracks and the sidecars at the top of the download, then used to delete the whole download folder. Anything else inside a subfolder went with it: a booklet PDF in `Extras`, the cover in each disc folder, an archive volume. So did a top level cover that failed to copy. Those files now stay in the download folder, the log lists them, and everything that did reach the library is still cleaned up. Copy and Hardlink imports are unchanged.
