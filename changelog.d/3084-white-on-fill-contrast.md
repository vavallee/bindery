### Fixed
- **Readable white text on amber, green, blue and red buttons and badges** (#3084). The setup banner, the pending requests count, warning and rollback buttons, the selected filter pills, the Calendar's today marker, the duplicate keeper chip and several sky and red action buttons put white text on a fill as light as amber 500 or emerald 600, between 2.1 and 4.0:1 against the 4.5:1 that text needs, and red and sky buttons got lighter on hover. The amber, green and sky fills now use the 700 shade, and every one of them goes darker rather than lighter on hover, so white stays at 4.8:1 or better in both themes. A few of these buttons set no text colour and took the page's, so they now set white themselves. Thanks to francisrath for the report.
