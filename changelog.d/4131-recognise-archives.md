### Fixed
- **A download that holds only a zip or a 7z now says it is waiting for an unpacker** (#4131). Before, it was reported as "no book files found" and spent its import retries, because only `.rar` counted as an archive. A `.zip`, a `.7z` and a split `.7z.001` are now recognised like a `.rar`: the Queue names the archive and its type, no retries are spent, and the archive stays out of the library in copy and hardlink modes. Bindery still does not unpack archives, so use unpackerr or your download client for that. Unpacking a zip in Bindery is planned separately. `.cbz` and `.epub` files are untouched. Thanks to @BraynArts for the report.
