### Fixed
- **Author sync now honors `minPages` and `skipMissingIsbn`** — both metadata profile settings previously had no effect during cataloging (only the fields were persisted, nothing consumed them, per #1980). Works are now checked against a real edition lookup: those whose editions all fall below the configured page-count floor, or that carry no ISBN when `skipMissingIsbn` is enabled, are filtered out of author sync/refresh. A work with no page data at all is treated as unknown, not zero, and passes the page-count floor either way. The edition lookup only runs for works that already survived every free filter, only when one of these settings is actually on, and is batched across surviving candidates with bounded concurrency rather than fired one at a time — so authors on profiles that touch neither setting pay no extra cost, and authors who do enable one don't add another serial-round-trip bottleneck to author sync (#1929). `AuthorSyncSummary` gains `skippedMinPages`/`skippedMissingIsbn` counts alongside the existing skip counters so drops stay visible.
