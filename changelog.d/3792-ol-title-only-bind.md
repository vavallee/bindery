### Fixed
- **New releases no longer get mixed up with an older book of the same name** (#3792). When OpenLibrary and Hardcover each listed a different book under the same title, a refresh could merge them into one row. Stephen King and Peter Straub's 2026 novel "Other Worlds Than These" ended up on the 2011 anthology of that name, with its cover and date, so it was filed as backlist and never wanted. Bindery now keeps two same titled books apart when their release dates are more than ten years apart. Rows already merged this way need a manual fix. Thanks sladester for the detailed report.
