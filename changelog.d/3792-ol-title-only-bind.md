### Fixed
- **New releases no longer get folded into an anthology with the same title** (#3792). Stephen King and Peter Straub's 2026 novel "Other Worlds Than These" landed on the 2011 anthology of the same name, which OpenLibrary also lists under King because he has a story in it. The novel took the anthology's cover and date, got filed as backlist and was never wanted. With an English only profile it could be dropped altogether, because OpenLibrary's edition sample called it Portuguese. A refresh now keeps a same titled work apart when the other one is an anthology the author only contributed to: many more credits than the author's own book, and the author nowhere near the front, whether or not you own it. Release dates and co-author order never split a book, so duplicate OpenLibrary records of one book still become a single book. A book matched to Hardcover also stores Hardcover's language when Hardcover finds an edition in one of your languages and the book doesn't already have one of them. Libraries already hit by this recover on the next author refresh: the novel is added as its own book if the author takes new books, and, if you don't own the anthology, its OpenLibrary and Hardcover links move over to it. The anthology may still be linked to the novel's series, which you can remove on the book page. Thanks sladester for the detailed report.
