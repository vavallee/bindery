### Fixed
- **New releases no longer get folded into an older book with the same title** (#3792). Stephen King and Peter Straub's 2026 novel "Other Worlds Than These" landed on the 2011 anthology of the same name, which OpenLibrary also lists under King, so it took the anthology's cover and date, got filed as backlist and was never wanted. With an English only profile it could be dropped altogether, because OpenLibrary's edition sample called it Portuguese. A refresh now keeps two same titled works apart when their own catalogue gives them different ids and their release dates are more than ten years apart, and a book matched to Hardcover takes Hardcover's language evidence over OpenLibrary's sample. Libraries already hit by this recover on the next author refresh: the novel stops being treated as the anthology and is added as its own book if the author takes new books. The anthology row may still be linked to the novel's series, which you can remove on the book page. Thanks sladester for the detailed report.
