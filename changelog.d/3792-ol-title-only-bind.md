### Fixed
- **New releases no longer get folded into an older book with the same title** (#3792). Stephen King and Peter Straub's 2026 novel "Other Worlds Than These" landed on the 2011 anthology of the same name, which OpenLibrary also lists under King, so it took the anthology's cover and date, got filed as backlist and was never wanted. With an English only profile it could be dropped altogether, because OpenLibrary's edition sample called it Portuguese. A refresh now keeps a book that Hardcover matched apart from a same titled work its catalogue lists separately, when their release dates are more than ten years apart and you don't own the other one. Plain duplicate OpenLibrary records of one book, like the several Hobbits, still become a single book. A book matched to Hardcover also takes Hardcover's language when Hardcover finds an edition in one of your languages. Libraries already hit by this recover on the next author refresh: the novel is added as its own book if the author takes new books, and its OpenLibrary and Hardcover links move over to it. The anthology may still be linked to the novel's series, which you can remove on the book page. Thanks sladester for the detailed report.
