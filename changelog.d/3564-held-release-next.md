### Fixed
- **Automatic search tries the next release when another book holds the best one** (#3564). When two books want the same release, for example the same work under two author rows or in two accounts, the scheduled search used to stop at a release another book was already downloading or had imported and leave the book wanted. It now passes that release over and grabs the next approved one. Pending releases are also kept per book, so a release held back by a delay profile for one book is held for the other book too instead of being folded into the first book's entry.
