### Fixed
- **Importing from Calibre no longer creates a second copy of a series that already exists** from Audiobookshelf or a metadata sync (#2173, thanks gavinmcfall). The Calibre import now files a book into the existing series of the same title when that series holds books by the same author or none yet, preferring one that already holds the author's books. A same titled series holding only other authors' books is left alone, as is an empty one linked on Hardcover to another author, and sub series like "Discworld (Witches)" and "Discworld (City Watch)" stay apart. Those duplicate copies were what made the Audiobookshelf import log `ambiguous existing series match` and skip the Hardcover series link. Copies an earlier import already made stay until you merge them on the Series page.
