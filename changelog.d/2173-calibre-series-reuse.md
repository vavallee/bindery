### Fixed
- **Importing from Calibre no longer creates a second copy of a series that already exists** from Audiobookshelf or a metadata sync (#2173, thanks gavinmcfall). The Calibre import now files a book into the existing series of the same title when that series holds books by the same author or none yet, and later imports go straight to it. A same titled series holding only other authors' books is left alone. Those duplicate copies were what made the Audiobookshelf import log `ambiguous existing series match` and skip the Hardcover series link. Copies an earlier import already made stay until you merge them on the Series page.
