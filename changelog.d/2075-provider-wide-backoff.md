### Fixed
- When a metadata provider asks Bindery to slow down during a bulk author import, every request to that provider now waits, not just the one that was refused (#2075). Each request used to back off on its own, so the other catalogue fetches and edition lookups in flight kept hitting OpenLibrary while it was throttling, and the 429s turned into timeouts and refused connections. A search whose wait would run past its own time limit now fails straight away rather than sitting out the timeout, while CSV, Readarr and Goodreads imports wait the pause out and carry on instead of failing rows. If the provider keeps refusing, the import stops after a few rows and tells you to try again later, rather than waiting and asking for every row. The ISBN and ASIN lookups in the Add Book dialog now give up after 20 seconds with an error instead of hanging until the connection drops. Thanks to TheOpsMgr for the report and the patient retesting.
