### Fixed
- **Books from a Hardcover list sync reach Calibre with their ISBN and publisher** (#1853). Since list sync stopped fetching every book's editions up front, a list synced book had no editions on record, so when it was later downloaded and handed to Calibre it went without an ISBN, publisher, publish date or edition language. Now a Hardcover book with no editions has them fetched from Hardcover when it is delivered to Calibre, for that one book only, and they are kept for next time. It needs a Hardcover API token. At most five books are fetched per delivery run or pull listing, so a large backlog arrives over a few minutes, and the book still goes to Calibre without them if Hardcover cannot answer.
