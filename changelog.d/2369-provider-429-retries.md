### Fixed
- Google Books, DNB, Nasjonalbiblioteket, Audible and audnex no longer give up on the first "too many requests" answer (#2369). They now wait and retry the way OpenLibrary does, honouring the provider's own `Retry-After`, so an Audiobookshelf import or a Google Books quota blip no longer drops a lookup that a second try would have answered. A refusal that outlasts the retries is reported as a rate limit, so scheduled discovery pauses instead of walking on through its queue. A Google Books key whose daily quota has run out is not retried, since that only resets the next day.
