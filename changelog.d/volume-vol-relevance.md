### Fixed
- **A book titled "Volume 17" now finds releases named "Vol 17"**: search used to drop the right release whenever the book and the release spelled the volume differently (Volume, Vol, Vols, Volumes). It works both ways now, and a release for a different volume number is still turned away. A format note in the middle of a title, such as "(Light Novel)" or "(Manga)" in "The Rising of the Shield Hero (Light Novel) Vol. 17", is also left out of the search and the match, since release names never carry it and some indexers returned nothing because of it. Thanks Fear Vector for the report.
