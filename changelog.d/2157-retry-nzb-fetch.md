### Fixed
- **A momentary network failure fetching an NZB no longer fails the grab for good** (#2157) — SABnzbd and NZBGet grabs fetch the NZB from the indexer themselves, and a single failed attempt marked the download failed and dropped the book back to Wanted, where it looked exactly like a book no release had been found for. Nothing retried it until the next scheduled search. The fetch is now attempted up to three times, with a short backoff, for failures another attempt could plausibly clear — a timeout, a dropped connection, a body that stops short. Failures that will not change are still reported immediately and after a single request: an indexer refusing the download, a rate-limit rejection, a blocked URL. Only the fetch retries; the upload to the download client is still made once, so a retry can never produce a duplicate job.
