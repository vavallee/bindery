### Security
- **Series now follow the books you can see.** With per user ownership on, a series that only links to another account's books no longer showed up as a blank row for everyone else. It used to appear in the series list and open by id with its title and Hardcover link, even though none of its books were visible. Now an account only sees a series that has at least one of its own books or an unowned shared one, and a series it cannot see answers not found, the same as a series that does not exist. The list total follows the same rule. A series with no books at all is only visible to admins. Admins and installs with ownership off see everything as before.
