### Changed
- **Edition wrappers no longer become series cards** (#3317). Hardcover sometimes files an omnibus, a boxed set, a coloring book or a run of single comic issues as a series of one, and Bindery used to show each one as a series. Those references are now skipped when books are synced, so the book just isn't linked to the fake series and keeps its real one if it has one. Series you already have stay until you merge or leave them, and rebinding a book to an omnibus keeps the real series it already had. A wrapper you own on its own (a complete collection) is no longer linked to the series, and it no longer auto monitors through series mode. Thanks to condensed69 for the report.
