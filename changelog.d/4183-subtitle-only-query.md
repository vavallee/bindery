### Fixed
- **Books titled "Series, Book N: Subtitle" now find releases named by the subtitle alone** (#4183). A book such as `Path to Transcendence, Book 1: Will of Mana` stayed Wanted on indexers that need every query word in the release name (MyAnonamouse through Prowlarr), because every query carried the series and the position and the release is named `Will of Mana by Ryan Arelius`. When a search ends without a relevant result, Bindery now sends one more query per indexer made of the author's surname and the subtitle (`Arelius Will of Mana`). A result from it is kept only when its name is the subtitle and the author plus a narrator or co-author credit, and known format, language, publisher or genre tags in brackets, so another author's book with the same title, a longer title (`Will of Mana Rising`), a numbered or series note, or another language is not picked up. It is sent only when the part before the colon ends in a position number and the subtitle is specific enough (not `A Novel` or `Book Two`), it counts against the indexer's daily query cap, and it shows in the search debug panel as `subtitleQuery`. Thanks to huepotman for the report and the indexer logs.
