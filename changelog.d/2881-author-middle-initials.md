### Fixed
- **Authors whose middle initials differ are no longer matched as one person** (#2881). "J. R. Smith" and "J. T. Smith" used to count as a confident match, so an import or a provider lookup could file one author's books under the other. Names whose initials disagree at the same position now need a person to confirm them. A name that only leaves an initial out ("J. Rowling" against "J.K. Rowling") is no longer matched on the name alone, but still matches wherever the book's title already agrees. The usual spellings of one author still match on their own: "J.R.R. Tolkien", "J. R. R. Tolkien", "JRR Tolkien", "Jrr Tolkien" and "John Ronald Reuel Tolkien" are still the same person.
