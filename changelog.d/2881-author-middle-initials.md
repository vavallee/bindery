### Fixed
- **Authors whose middle initials differ are no longer matched as one person** (#2881). "J. R. Smith" and "J. T. Smith" used to count as a confident match, so an import or a provider lookup could file one author's books under the other. Names whose initials disagree at the same position now need a person to confirm them. A name that only leaves initials out, whether the first name is an initial ("J. Rowling" against "J.K. Rowling") or written in full ("George Martin" against "George R. R. Martin"), is not matched on the name alone, but does match wherever the book's title already agrees. The usual spellings of one author still match on their own: "J.R.R. Tolkien", "J. R. R. Tolkien", "JRR Tolkien", "Jrr Tolkien" and "John Ronald Reuel Tolkien" are still the same person.
