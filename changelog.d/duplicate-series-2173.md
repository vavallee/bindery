### Added
- **Find and merge duplicate series in bulk** (#2173). Libraries that ran both the Calibre and the Audiobookshelf import often hold the same series twice, and the Audiobookshelf import can't link a Hardcover series to items whose series title matches two rows. The Series page now tells admins how many titles appear more than once and lists them with book counts, authors and Hardcover links. Merge a group through the usual preview, or merge every group whose authors match in one go after a dry run summary. Groups with different authors are left for you to check by hand. Any merge also lets the next normal Audiobookshelf import retry the items it reported as `seriesLinkFailed`, so no full import is needed. Thanks gavinmcfall for the report.
