### Fixed
- **ABS import stops asking Hardcover about series it cannot link** (#2173). When two series in your library share a name, usually one from the Calibre import and one from the ABS import, the Hardcover series link used to give up and the item was searched again on every run. Now the series that already holds the book wins, and when neither does the item is remembered and retried on the same monthly schedule as a miss instead of every night. These failed links also show up as `seriesLinkFailed` in the import summary instead of hiding behind `failed: 0`.
