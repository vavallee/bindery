### Fixed
- **OpenLibrary works take their language and cover from the edition OpenLibrary features** (#1779). When a work had no language or cover of its own, Bindery read a handful of editions in whatever order OpenLibrary returned them, so a work whose first few happened to be translations could be filed as foreign language or shown with a translated cover. It now uses the work's featured edition, the one its openlibrary.org page shows, and only samples other editions for what that edition lacks, preferring a cover in the language it settled on. A single book fetched from OpenLibrary, as on Re-bind, also takes the featured edition's cover when the work has none. This costs up to two extra OpenLibrary requests per work that needs sampling, and one extra request for a single book only when its work has no cover.
