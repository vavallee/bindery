### Added
- **Soulseek results in interactive search** (#1717). With a slskd client enabled, interactive search now also searches Soulseek and lists what peers offer beside your indexer results. Each result carries a Soulseek badge, the peer's user name, how many files the folder holds, and whether the peer has a free slot or how long its queue is. Grab is not enabled yet: the button is disabled on Soulseek results and says grabbing arrives in a later release, so nothing is downloaded. The automatic search for wanted books does not use Soulseek. It stays off unless you have added and enabled a slskd client. The title and the author's last name you search for leave your machine as search text on the Soulseek network, and slskd runs one search at a time, so a search that includes Soulseek usually takes about 15 seconds longer. Bindery waits at most 20 seconds for slskd, then shows your indexer results without it.
