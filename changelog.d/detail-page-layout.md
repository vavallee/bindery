### Changed
- **Author and Book detail pages restructured** — both now share one container width, so moving between them no longer collapses and re-aligns the content. The author page's run-on stats line becomes a fixed four-cell strip, its eight-control action row becomes five with the rest behind a **More** menu, and its three chip groups become three selects that finally expose the `downloading` and `skipped` filters. The book page shows series name and position, clamps long descriptions with show more/less, moves **Edit** into the header where the metadata is, and reserves solid red for the one irreversible action.
- **Books without cover art now get a real placeholder** — title set large over a colour derived from the book, stable per book, instead of a flat grey box that read as a broken image.
