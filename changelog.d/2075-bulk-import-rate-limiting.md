### Fixed
- **Bulk author import no longer storms OpenLibrary with unbounded requests** (#2075) — a CSV or Readarr-DB import fired one goroutine per author with no cap, so a 19-author CSV opened 19 simultaneous catalogue fetches; OpenLibrary throttles per-UA, and 429s cascaded into timeouts and connection refusals with nothing to slow the batch down. Catalogue fetches for newly-imported authors are now bounded to 2 at a time and paced 3 seconds apart — each author's own fetch already fans out further internally, so 2 keeps the realistic worst case well below what used to trip the throttle — and this pacing is what actually prevents the storm. The OpenLibrary client additionally retries an occasional 429/502/503/504 or transient network failure (honoring `Retry-After` when the response sends one, exponential backoff otherwise) instead of failing the request outright — a safety net for a stray hiccup, not a substitute for the pacing above.
