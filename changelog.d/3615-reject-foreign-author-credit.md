### Fixed
- **Automatic search no longer grabs another author's book, or a different book by the same author, for a title that is not out yet** (#3615). A release that credits someone else with "by" is now rejected even when its name does not start with the wanted title, as in "The Last Ride by Nick Louth" for "Last Ride" or "Smoke & Mirrors by E A Copen" for "Smoke and Mirrors". A release that carries only the part of the title before the colon and then goes on into a different title credited to the same author, such as "Halo: Oblivion by Troy Denning" for "Halo: Fireteam Noble", is rejected too.
