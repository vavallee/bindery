### Security
- **History, Refresh all and recommendations stay within each user's library** (#3587). With `BINDERY_ENFORCE_TENANCY` on, deleting a book used to leave its history (release names, indexers and import paths) visible to every user, and any user could delete those entries or blocklist their releases. History entries now belong to an account and stay with it after the book is gone; entries with no owner and no book are admin only. A user who is not an admin could also run Refresh all over every account's authors and read the instance's author count, and their Discover recommendations were built from, and could name, other users' books and the admin's Hardcover want to read list. Refresh all now covers only that user's own authors and reports only their run, and recommendations use only their own and shared books. Recommendations saved before upgrading are cleared once for every account that is not an admin and rebuilt on the next refresh; dismissed books and excluded authors are kept. Otherwise admins and installs with tenancy off are unchanged.
