### Fixed
- **The automatic search now gets to every wanted book, not just the first ones by title** (#4185). The scheduled sweep searched the wanted list in the same title order every run and kept no record of what it had searched. When an indexer's daily query limit ran out part way through, or Bindery restarted during a sweep, the next sweep started again from the top, so on a large wanted list the first titles were searched every time and the rest never. The sweep now searches books it has never searched first and then the ones it searched longest ago, and remembers that across restarts, so a list too big for one day's allowance is covered over several days. A book counts as searched once its search reached at least one indexer. One that every indexer skipped keeps its place at the front. Searching a book yourself or adding it with auto-grab on counts as well, so the sweep does not repeat it straight away. The first sweep after the upgrade runs in the old title order. The Wanted page, OPDS and every other list keep their order.
