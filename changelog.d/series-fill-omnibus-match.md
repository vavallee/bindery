### Fixed
- **Series Fill no longer lets a box-set/omnibus title silently satisfy a real book's catalog slot.** `TitleScore`'s substring-aware components (`PartialRatio`/`TokenSetRatio`) score a target title fully contained in a much longer omnibus title as a perfect match — tying an actual exact-title match at the same score. Fill's candidate matcher took the first title over the confidence threshold rather than the best one, so an omnibus record could win that tie and get linked to a slot the real book should have filled, leaving the real book silently unmatched with no error surfaced. Fill now keeps the best-scoring candidate and breaks same-score ties by title-length closeness to the target, reliably preferring the real book over the longer omnibus/box-set title it happens to contain.
