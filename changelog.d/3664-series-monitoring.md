### Added
- **Monitoring a series now finds and adds its new books** (#2523). Requested by magrhino, who also proposed the Upcoming behaviour. Turn on Settings > General > Series checking (off by default, because the Monitored switch did nothing until now and every series already switched on would start adding books) and Bindery checks each monitored series against its Hardcover link on a schedule. It adds what is missing the way Fill gaps does, so box sets and split editions are left out, and it leaves a row alone when it cannot tell it from a book you already have. What a series adds is set on its card: Upcoming only (the default) adds announced and newly released books and leaves older gaps to Fill gaps, All adds every missing book. A book with a future release date is listed as Upcoming on the Wanted page, which also gains an Upcoming filter, and the scheduled search leaves it alone until that date. Each check follows a moved date. The series card shows when it was last checked and how many books were added, or why it could not be checked, such as a missing Hardcover link.
